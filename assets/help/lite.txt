HEARTHTABLE LITE 1.0.1 · THE DM'S TABLE

Lite is a separate, DM-led virtual tabletop for original 2014 fifth edition. The DM decides outcomes and homebrew exceptions. Players move their own pawns on the shared board and use their character sheet for rolls and intentions.

DM: PREPARE THE TABLE
The board starts blacked out to players. You still see and edit the complete scene. Drag any miniature wherever you want, including through walls, occupied squares or beyond its speed. The ruler and live drag caption show distance using the scene's grid scale and diagonal setting. Shift-drag rotates, right-drag pans, and the wheel zooms. DM placement ignores paths and movement budgets. Player moves follow revealed walkable routes, with no distance cap outside combat. In Initiative, the DM begins/ends combat and advances the private order: players then move only on their turn, spending a cumulative speed allowance. The DM can set/reset that allowance for Dash, corrections or homebrew. Walls/closed doors and unrevealed squares block player routes. Only the DM opens/closes doors. There are no automatic encounters, attacks, saves or spell outcomes.

Use Build / F2 to place objects, walls and terrain, or Scenes to import a map / self-contained .hearthscene. Grid configures scale, calibration, manual fog and optional room concealment. Visibility conceals/reveals individual objects. Select any creature to conceal/reveal it or edit its AC, abilities, HP, attacks and sheet. Newly added enemies are concealed until you reveal them.

SPELL AREAS
Drag Circle, Cone or Square to mark an area. Choose Erase area and click the area to remove it; where areas overlap, the newest one is removed first. Areas… lists every spell area with an individual Remove control and Clear all spell areas. Ctrl+Z restores a removed area. Players cannot erase the DM’s areas. These are visual guides; the DM still resolves effects.

CREATE YOUR HERO
Create hero opens seven guided steps: Identity, Attributes, Skills, Equipment, Magic, Miniature and Review. Back/Next buttons stay visible; the numbered step buttons let you revisit a section. Required identity choices and point-buy errors take you back to the relevant choice before submission.

Choose class, ancestry/subrace, subclass (when available), background and alignment from lists. New class choices replace the draft’s starting sheet, so choose class before later customization. The included ancestry/subclass options follow the 2014 SRD; imported and DM-approved names remain available. Name, description and story are free text; missing choices can be added through Add a DM-approved choice.

Standard array is recommended: choosing an assigned score swaps it with the other ability, so each is used once. Point buy gives 27 points with the 2014 costs. Ancestry bonuses are displayed separately. Manual / imported scores are final and never receive ancestry bonuses twice. New heroes can use calculated basic HP/AC/attack; imported heroes keep their printed totals unless you explicitly enable calculation. Review higher-level features, proficiency, armor restrictions, subclass benefits and exceptions with your DM.

Select skill training, weapons, armor, equipment and spells from the lists. Suggested training combines class/background choices for review; choose replacements for overlaps with your DM. Spells default to your class and available spell levels; other-class or exceptional choices need DM approval. Gear has quantity/equipped/attuned records; a pack is recorded as one item until you review its contents/weight. Customize the live miniature, then review and confirm the complete sheet. Imports use this same flow without replacing their scores, bonuses, custom choices, equipment or spell selections. Cancel leaves the campaign untouched.

BLACKOUT AND FOG
BLACK OUT player board hides the whole tabletop while leaving each player's character sheet usable. Revealing the board preserves individual hidden creatures, concealed objects and fog. Reveal / Conceal paints a 3-by-3-square fog brush; Visibility can cover or clear the whole map. Player preview shows the obscured view without changing your own authoritative data.

Enemy HP, AC, abilities, private notes, initiative totals and initiative order are withheld from player snapshots. Hidden actors and concealed props are removed. Fogged geometry and imported map pixels are removed/masked before transfer. Blackout transfers an empty placeholder scene and owned sheets. Information already shown to someone cannot be taken back from their memory or previously downloaded copies.

SESSION
Open Session, choose a UDP port and passphrase, and host. Friends need the same Hearthtable Lite build. Assign their heroes or review their submitted character / filled D&D Beyond PDF. Players may create a hero, review all imported values, customize the miniature and submit it for DM acceptance. Starting the table allows player intentions. LAN/internet routing uses the existing manual UDP port-forwarding / trusted virtual-LAN workflow; there is no relay or D&D Beyond account login.

PLAYERS: EXPAND YOUR SHEET
Click Expand character sheet (or C). Overview contains final abilities, saves, all 18 skills, passive senses, HP/temp HP, AC, speed, proficiency, initiative modifier, inspiration, defenses, conditions, exhaustion, Hit Dice and death-save records. Actions contains weapon rolls and the 2014 action references, including grapple, shove, Help, Dodge, Hide, Ready, Search, objects, bonus actions and reactions.

Spells contains your spellbook, preparation, slots, casting ability, save DC, attack bonus and complete included spell text: casting time, range, duration, components, concentration, rituals, area, damage and higher levels. Equipment contains items, quantity, weight, equipped/attuned state and five coin types. Features contains limited-use counters and class/race/feat reference notes. Description, Notes and Extras record origin, multiclass notes, appearance, proficiencies, languages, senses, personality/ideals/bonds/flaws, backstory, allies, organizations, treasure, companions, mounts and homebrew.

This independent sheet covers these categories; it does not include D&D Beyond accounts, paid books, its character progression engine or a complete licensed feature database. Included spell/action information uses the 2014 SRD 5.1. Add non-SRD and homebrew material as text / custom spells with the DM. Character progression, multiclass slots, equipment effects and exceptions are editable and reviewed manually.

ROLLS AND INTENTIONS
Checks, saves, attacks, damage, death saves and Hit Dice roll only dice; they do not apply success, failure, HP or resource changes. Use Normal / Advantage / Disadvantage as directed by the DM. Custom dice accept mixed terms such as 2d6+1d4+3. A d20 check rolls two d20s and keeps the higher/lower when selected. The DM's rolls are private. Player rolls go only to that player and the DM. Physical dice results can be described in an intention.

The DM prompts initiative, saves, skills, ability checks or death saves. A prompt appears on the player's sheet. Initiative rolls do not sort the table; the DM privately adds pieces to any desired order and uses the arrows to rearrange it. Begin combat enables own-turn movement limits; End combat restores unlimited-distance exploration. Advance turn refreshes the next pawn’s movement, without resolving actions or effects. Announce whose turn it is reveals only that chosen announcement.

Propose an attack, spell, feature, item, rest or improvised action from the sheet. Choose visible proposed targets and describe your intention, area/origin, components and exceptions. Nothing is applied or spent by submitting. The DM's Inbox displays the rule text, saving throw, DC, damage expression, area, components and player notes. The DM may roll proposed damage privately, then choose each affected creature, full/half damage, healing, conditions, amounts and resource spending. Approve applies only those explicit choices; Decline applies nothing. Damage uses temporary HP first and half damage rounds down. The DM applies resistances, criticals, concentration, death, riders and all homebrew choices deliberately. Do not assume those effects are automatic.

CUSTOM CREATURES
Use + Custom creature to write a stat block, or import a .hearthcreature. To ask Codex for a bespoke creature, provide its appearance/reference, size, AC, HP, abilities, attacks and traits, and ask for a Hearthtable Lite creature package. It can contain bounded box/sphere/cylinder/cone/torus geometry, colors and transforms. Scripts, shaders and arbitrary resource paths are not accepted. An original Ember Warden example is included in the source's assets/creatures folder. Custom geometry and sheets travel with saves and portable campaigns.

SAVES, PORTABLE COPIES AND UPDATES
Lite uses its own Hearthtable Lite user-data directory and protocol 11. The original Hearthtable remains a separate edition. To bring an old campaign into Lite, export its JSON and import it through Campaign; keep the original backup. Portable campaign copies contain private DM notes, hidden scenes and the active installed application code, so keep those copies private.

Download Hearthtable_Lite_Portable.exe once. Later compatible Lite code/assets updates use Settings → Import application update with Hearthtable-Lite-VERSION.hearthupdate from the lite-vVERSION release. Settings verifies the published release digest, saves a campaign backup and retains rollback before restarting. Classic Hearthtable and Lite updates are not interchangeable. Engine/updater-foundation changes may require a new EXE.

Build validation uses Godot 4.5.1 on Linux, rendered software OpenGL, exported Windows resources, and actual local DM/two-player UDP sessions. Native Windows, audio, real GPU hardware and external residential internet play require separate testing; this release remains a prerelease. Existing source and in-app notices retain SRD, engine, font and model attribution.
